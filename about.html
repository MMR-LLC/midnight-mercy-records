About the label

Hope found in the darkest hour.

Midnight Mercy Records creates honest, faith-centered music born from real struggles and anchored in the hope of Jesus Christ.

Our mission

Make music that helps people feel seen before it asks them to feel strong.

We believe some of the most powerful encounters with God happen in life’s darkest hours. The label exists to create music for the anxious, grieving, exhausted, doubting, and searching.

Midnight Mercy Records is building a home for artists and stories that carry honesty, emotional weight, spiritual depth, and hope.
