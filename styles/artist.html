The mission

A voice for the battles people rarely talk about.

Ashan Levi exists to create honest faith-centered music about anxiety, grief, spiritual warfare, numbness, surrender, redemption, and the hope found in Jesus Christ.

The songs do not pretend that faith removes every struggle. They are written from inside the tension—when prayer feels difficult, when the night is long, and when choosing to believe is an act of endurance.
