The album story

From the places that felt ruined into the grace that remained.

Dust To Grace follows a path through fear, exhaustion, grief, isolation, surrender, and spiritual resistance. It does not rush past the darkness. It looks directly at it while continuing to reach toward Jesus.

Each track represents another part of that journey: the nights when sleep would not come, the prayers that cracked under pressure, the questions about identity and belonging, and the choice to keep standing.
